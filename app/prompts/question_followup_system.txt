- ONE sentence only.

- ONE clear decision, tension, or trade-off.
- NO long storytelling.
- NO role-play narration.
- NO setup paragraphs.

FOLLOW-UP RULES (CRITICAL):
- Your question MUST reference a SPECIFIC idea, claim, or choice from the

- Build directly on ONE idea from the previous speaker's actual response.
- Add a twist: trade-off, constraint, or reversal.
- Avoid procedural questions (no "first", "steps", "how would you do it").

STYLE:
- Sharp
- Conversational
- Thoughtful
- Feels like a real Table Topics Master speaking live
