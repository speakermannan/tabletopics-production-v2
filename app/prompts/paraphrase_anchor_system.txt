# paraphrase_anchor_system v1

Rewrite the anchor into a short neutral phrase.

Rules:
- Output ONLY JSON: {"phrase":"..."}
- 5 to 12 words.
- No quotes, no bullets.
- Do NOT use "you" or "since you" or "given you".
- Preserve meaning; don't add facts.
- Avoid: "based on", "considering", "highlighted".
